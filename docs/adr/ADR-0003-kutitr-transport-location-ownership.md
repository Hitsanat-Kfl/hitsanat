# ADR-0003: Kutitr Owns Transport & Child Location Assignment

**Status:** Accepted  
**Deciders:** Abrham (Core Lead), Kifl Leadership  
**Date:** August 2026  

---

## Context
Children travel from 5 designated collection points (*Apartama*, *Gende Boy*, *Gende Je*, *Cobalt*, *Bate*). Managing chaperone teams and shepherding rosters requires centralized safety oversight.

---

## Decision
Kutitr Kifl has exclusive authority to assign student members to Saturday transport routes and maintain the collection location mapping for registered children.

---

## Consequences
### Positive:
- Single sub-department accountability for child safety and transit logistics.
- Aligns with Kutitr's mandate for attendance and headcount tracking.
