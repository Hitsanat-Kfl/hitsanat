# Planning System: Monthly & Weekly Planning Detailed Specs

## Hitsanat Kifl Children's Ministry Management System
**Document Version:** 2.1  

---

## 1. Monthly Planning Mechanics

Monthly plans translate quarterly goals into concrete milestone targets for the 9 active academic months (Tikimt through Sene).

### Monthly Breakdown Model:
- **`ethiopian_month`:** Target month (`Tikimt`, `Hidar`, etc.).
- **`target_quantity`:** Planned numeric output (e.g., 4 Saturday classes, 2 Awdemerit presentations, 8 choir rehearsals).
- **`planned_budget_disbursement`:** Allocated portion of activity budget.

---

## 2. Weekly Planning Mechanics & Session Linking

Weekly plan tasks represent the atomic unit of ministry work:
1. **Task Description:** Specific actionable item (e.g., "Teach Ge'ez Alphabet Part 1 to Kutr 1").
2. **Session Date:** Linked to a specific Saturday or Sunday date.
3. **Assigned Servant Team:** Minimum 2 student members.
4. **Resources & Equipment:** Required materials (e.g. puppets, sound system, booklets).
